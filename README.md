# Kristyn Rostan - Executive Portfolio Website

Welcome to the personal portfolio website of **Kristyn Rostan**, Senior Leader in Commercial Operations, CRM Architecture, and Logistics Transformation.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Sections
- **Executive Hero**: Core background, key specialties, and quick contact buttons.
- **About Kristyn**: Professional narrative, transformation philosophy, and impact metrics.
- **Core Focus & Capabilities**: Detailed cards covering Commercial Operations, CRM Strategy, SOP & Process Design, Sales Enablement, Client Success, and Revenue Management.
- **Interactive Career Timeline**: Filterable chronological work history across:
  - **PGL (Perimeter Global Logistics)**
  - **SEKO Logistics**
  - **Expeditors International**
  - **Hamilton Partners**
- **Technology & Tools**: Microsoft Dynamics 365, Salesforce, Power BI, CargoWise, Logixboard, NEO, Magaya, WebCargo, and EDI systems.
- **Credentials & Education**: Janek Performance Group certifications (5x), Lean Enterprise training, CSU, and 2018 SEKO Service Excellence Award.
- **Direct Contact**: Interactive contact section with one-click email copy and direct links.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & Vanilla CSS3**: Clean, responsive layout with dual Dark/Light mode theme toggle.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Interactive timeline filtering, responsive navigation, and copy actions.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Lightweight, fast-loading, and portable.

---

## 🚀 How to View Locally
Simply double-click [`index.html`](./index.html) in your file explorer to open the website directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
